Key and Wallet Backup and Full Sync Instructions:

Before making direct changes to the chain store, backup your keys and wallet.
GUI Wallet -> File -> Export all private keys
Shut down the GUI Wallet, which will also bring down 'zerod'. You can also check in Task Manager, respectively zerowallet.exe and zerod.exe (Zero node with a JSON-RPC server)

On my Windows 10 machine working wallet.zero is in C:\Users\Owner\AppData\Roaming\zero

Copy it to a flash drive or two, and to another name in the same directory. Also grab zero.conf and zeronode.conf.  For further investigation, copy debug.log or rename it. Those get rather large after a while, and I often ZIP them.

To get a full fresh chain download, move to another directory or simply delete folders blocks and chainstate.  You'll need at least 8GB of space to download and validate current chain. Depending on the speed and the quality of your Internet, it could take 7-12 hours.

You could run zerod.exe directly during sync or simply restart GUI Wallet, which starts zerod.  It takes many minutes for the entire process to start fully, before the sync gets far enough for GUI to start reporting % download.

If you want to track progress directly, check debug.log file. I prefer a brand new one for an operational restart, which shows the startup-up process, then reports connections made and the blocks coming in. There are many messages logged, and some are formulated as errors, but worry not.
